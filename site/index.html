How it works

From a paper form to a signed PDF in three steps

  1. Open the form

    Drop in a PDF, a scan or a photo from your phone. Several files open as one document, in the order you choose.

  2. Fill it in

    Pick Text, Tick, Cross, Date or Sign and click on the page. Move or resize anything, and undo every change.

  3. Export the final copy

    Chainpen points out boxes and blanks you might have missed, then makes a PDF that can’t be changed. That’s the one to send.

Features

Made for forms without fillable fields

Many forms are scans, photos or flat PDFs with nothing to click into. Chainpen works on any page, including a photo of a form someone handed you.

Sign with a photo of your signature

Sign a piece of paper, take a photo, and Chainpen removes the paper and its lines so only the ink remains. You can also draw with a mouse, trackpad or pen, or type your name in a handwriting style.

Keep a signature for next time and it’s encrypted with your computer’s keychain. On Linux without a keyring, Chainpen tells you before keeping one unencrypted.

Catch what you missed before you send

When you export, Chainpen lists boxes left unticked and empty blanks after labels like “Name:” or “Date:”. Show me takes you to each one with the right tool ready.

They’re hints: Chainpen can’t know what every form needs, so it still asks you to look over each page.

Correct pre-printed details for good

Cover a wrong detail with whiteout and type the right one on top. In the final copy the covered text is deleted, not just hidden, so nobody can copy it or uncover it.

Your working copy keeps everything, so you can still move the whiteout later.

Privacy

Your documents never leave your computer

Forms hold your name, address, bank details and signature. Chainpen doesn’t need any of it to leave your desk, so none of it does.

  • No account, no uploadOpen a file and start. There’s nothing to sign up for and no cloud copy.
  • You control saved signaturesSignatures you keep are encrypted with your computer’s keychain, and you can remove them at any time. On Linux without a keyring, Chainpen tells you before keeping one unencrypted.
  • The network is only used for updatesChainpen asks GitHub for the list of releases and, when there’s a newer version, downloads it. Nothing about you or your files is sent.
  • No telemetryCrash reports are off unless you turn them on, and even then you read each one before it’s sent.
  • Source you can readThe code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check these claims.

For scripts and AI assistants

Fill forms from the command line

The chainpen command lists a PDF’s fields and fills them from values or a JSON file. chainpen mcp lets AI assistants do the same over the Model Context Protocol, on your computer. Signing stays in the app.

Command line guide

# See what the form asks
chainpen fields application.pdf

# Fill it and lock the answers
chainpen fill application.pdf --out done.pdf \
    --set Name="Jordan Lee" \
    --set ContactEmail=yes \
    --set Heard=Friend

Opening Chainpen the first time

Chainpen isn’t signed with a paid developer certificate yet, so your computer doesn’t recognise who made it and asks you to confirm. Only do this for files from the Chainpen releases page.

Windows

  1. Open Chainpen-win-x64-Setup.exe from your downloads.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, select More info.
  3. Check that it names the same file, then select Run anyway.

Chainpen installs for you only, with no administrator password, and appears in the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Open Chainpen-osx-arm64-Setup.pkg from your Downloads folder.
  2. If macOS says it can’t be opened, select Done (not Move to Bin).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and select Open Anyway next to the Chainpen installer.
  4. Follow the installer. The first time you open Chainpen you may need to right-click it in Applications and choose Open.

Linux

  1. Right-click Chainpen-linux-x64.AppImage, choose Properties → Permissions, and tick Allow executing file as program.
  2. Double-click it to start Chainpen.
